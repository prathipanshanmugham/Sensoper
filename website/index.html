Questions people ask us

How much subsidy can I get?

For homes, PM Surya Ghar pays ₹30,000 for 1 kW, ₹60,000 for 2 kW and ₹78,000 for 3 kW or more. It is paid into your bank account after the system is installed and inspected. We file the application on the national portal for you.

What size system do I need?

Around Erode, each kW of panels makes about 4.4 units a day — roughly 130 units a month. Divide your monthly units by 130 for a starting size. The calculator above does this from your bill, and the site visit confirms it.

How much roof space does it take?

About 100 sq ft of shade-free roof for every kW. A 3 kW system needs about 300 sq ft. We mount panels on a raised structure so the terrace below stays usable.

Will I have power during a power cut?

An on-grid system switches off during a power cut, for the safety of the line workers. If you want backup, choose a hybrid system with a battery — it keeps chosen loads running.

How long does it take?

Installation itself takes a few days once materials are on site. The net meter and subsidy steps depend on the electricity board’s timelines; we follow them up and you can track each step in your customer login.

Do the panels need cleaning?

Yes — dust lowers generation, especially in the dry months. A rinse every couple of weeks keeps output up. Our AMC plans include scheduled cleaning and check-ups.

Can I see how my system is doing?

Yes. Every Sensoper customer can get a login to see their savings, units generated, CO₂ avoided, payments and service visits, and to raise a service request from their phone.
