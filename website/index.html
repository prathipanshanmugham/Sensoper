Four ways to run on sunshine

Every site is different. Here is what each kind of system does, who it suits and what goes into it.

On-grid rooftop

Cut the bill, sell back the extra.

Panels on your roof feed your home through an on-grid inverter. Extra units go to the TNEB grid through a net meter and come off your bill.

Best for
Homes, shops, offices and factories with a steady EB supply
What’s in it
TOPCon DCR panels, on-grid inverter, net meter, earthing and lightning arrester
Brands
Waaree, Vikram Solar, Polycab, SolarEdge
Subsidy
Up to ₹78,000 for homes under PM Surya Ghar
Good to know
It switches off in a power cut, to keep line workers safe

Off-grid

Your own power, no grid needed.

No connection to the grid at all. Panels charge a battery bank through an off-grid inverter, and your loads run from the battery day and night.

Best for
Farm houses, remote sites and places where the supply is weak or missing
What’s in it
Panels, off-grid inverter with an MPPT charge controller, battery bank
Brands
Asha Power, Torque Lithium
Good to know
The battery is sized to the hours you need covered — we work it out from your loads

Battery storage (BESS)

Keep running through every power cut.

A hybrid inverter and a lithium battery store the day’s extra sunshine. When the grid goes off, your chosen loads move to the battery on their own.

Best for
Homes with power cuts, and shops, clinics and factories that can’t stop or want to run less diesel
What’s in it
Hybrid inverter (or a power conversion system for big sites), LiFePO4 battery with its own management system
Brands
Asha Power Rover and ESS, Torque Lithium, Aviva
Good to know
Adds to an existing rooftop system, or charges from the grid on its own

Solar water pump

Water all day, without diesel.

Panels drive the pump through a pump controller. Water flows whenever the sun is up — no diesel, and no waiting for three-phase supply.

Best for
Farms with a borewell or an open well
What’s in it
Panels on a ground frame, solar pump controller, submersible or surface pump from 3 to 10 HP
Brands
Textro pump controllers

PM-KUSUM where you qualify — we check it for you

A day on solar, hour by hour

  1. MorningPanels wake up with the sun. The battery has carried the house through the night.
  2. MiddayThe roof makes far more than the house uses. The battery fills and the extra goes to the grid.
  3. EveningThe sun has gone and the lights and fans are on. The battery is doing the work.
  4. NightStill about two-thirds full. Tomorrow’s sun tops it up again.

Why is your EB bill so high?

TNEB charges more for each unit as your use goes up.

The first 100 units are free. Past 500 units, every unit costs ₹6.30 or more.

Solar panels make your own units on the roof, so the costliest units go first.

A 3 kW roof makes about 790 units every two months — enough for most homes.

Questions people ask us

Should I choose on-grid, off-grid or hybrid?

On-grid is the cheapest per unit and gets the PM Surya Ghar subsidy, but it switches off in a power cut. Hybrid adds a battery so chosen loads keep running in a power cut. Off-grid has no grid connection at all and suits places where the supply is weak or missing. We suggest one after the site visit, from your bill and how often the power goes.

How much subsidy can I get?

For homes, PM Surya Ghar pays ₹30,000 for 1 kW, ₹60,000 for 2 kW and ₹78,000 for 3 kW or more. It is paid into your bank account after the system is installed and inspected. We file the application on the national portal for you.

What size system do I need?

Around Erode, each kW of panels makes about 4.4 units a day — roughly 130 units a month. Divide your monthly units by 130 for a starting size. The calculator above does this from your bill, and the site visit confirms it.

How long does a lithium battery last?

LiFePO4 batteries are made for thousands of charge cycles — many years of daily use. The exact cycle life and warranty depend on the brand and model; we show them on your quote.

Can a solar pump run my existing borewell?

Usually, yes. We check the depth, the water level and the pump size, then match the panels and the pump controller to it. PM-KUSUM subsidy depends on eligibility — we check that too.

How much roof space does it take?

About 100 sq ft of shade-free roof for every kW. A 3 kW system needs about 300 sq ft. We mount panels on a raised structure so the terrace below stays usable.

How long does it take?

Installation itself takes a few days once materials are on site. The net meter and subsidy steps depend on the electricity board’s timelines; we follow them up and you can track each step in your customer login.

Can I see how my system is doing?

Yes. Every Sensoper customer can get a login to see their savings, units generated, CO₂ avoided, payments and service visits, and to raise a service request from their phone.
